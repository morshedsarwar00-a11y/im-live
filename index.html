📲 Add to Home Screen

Install Im Live as an app — it gets its own icon on your phone and opens full-screen, with no browser bars. No Play Store, no account, no cost.

On iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add— the Im Live icon appears on your Home Screen.

On Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menuat the top-right of Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app(or “Add to Home screen”).
  3. Tap Install— open Im Live any time from your Home Screen or app drawer.
